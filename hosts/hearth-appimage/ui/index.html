How it works

The app's files are unpacked (nothing in the package is run), its libraries are put inside, and it's packed into one AppImage that runs on its own.

Graphics drivers and the system's basics (glibc, sound, X11/Wayland) always come from this computer. Apps that need system services or drivers set up, or Python/Java apps, may only partly work.

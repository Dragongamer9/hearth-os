# The new Hearth Photos: a photo library, not just a viewer (approved Nov 15, 2026)

Mockups: next-update/photos-1-library.png, next-update/photos-2-photo.png, next-update/photos-3-albums-people-places.png, next-update/photos-4-controller.png, next-update/photos-5-edit.png
Release: 1.14

The owner's choice: the library look (1, 2, 3, 5) while you use a keyboard and mouse, the controller look (4) by itself
when a controller is used (the same idea as the new Settings). Everything (search, faces, what's in a photo) is worked
out on this PC; nothing leaves it. Opening a single picture from Files still opens it straight away.

## Library (photos-1)
- [ ] left list: Photos with its icon; All photos, Memories, Favorites, Videos, Game captures, People, Places, From your phone, Folders, Trash, each with its count; the picked one marked
- [ ] Your albums: each with its small picture
- [ ] the phone box: Chris's iPhone with its dot, "New photos come in by themselves · last 4 min ago", and photos · size · "all on this PC, nothing leaves it"
- [ ] search: "Search: sunset at the lake …" by what's in a photo, "Searched on this PC"
- [ ] Import, Select, Slideshow and the Size slider
- [ ] Memories: four cards (a year ago today, a trip, game captures, a season), "made on this PC from your own photos"
- [ ] the photos by day (Today, Yesterday, Earlier this week …) with the day, count and where they came from; rows of pictures at their own shapes
- [ ] on the pictures: ▶ and the length for videos, ♥ for favorites, a label for screenshots and game captures (which game, Steam)
- [ ] the controller's / keyboard's ember ring on the picked picture
- [ ] the date strip on the right: months, then years, to jump to

## A photo (photos-2)
- [ ] back, its name, when and "4 of 9"
- [ ] Favorite, Edit, Share, Add to album, Set as wallpaper, Info, Trash
- [ ] the picture large, ‹ › to the others, Fit / 100 % / Turn / Full screen
- [ ] the strip of the day's other pictures, the shown one marked
- [ ] Details: date and time, camera and its settings, size, file kind
- [ ] Place: a small map (OpenStreetMap) with the pin and the place's name
- [ ] People: the faces in it, "+ Name a face"
- [ ] Found in it: what's in the picture as tags, "worked out on this PC (your graphics card)"
- [ ] In albums, "+ Add to an album"
- [ ] where the file is, Show in Files

## Albums, people and places (photos-3)
- [ ] Collections with Albums / People / Places / Folders and + New album
- [ ] albums kept up to date by themselves: Game captures (Steam, Game Mode and Desktop), From your phone, Screenshots (this PC and your Ally), Videos, Favorites, Made by your AI (ComfyUI pictures), each with its count
- [ ] Your albums with their counts, and + New album; Wallpapers used by Hearth's wallpaper picker
- [ ] People: faces grouped on this PC, names only you see, Add a name, See all; turning faces off forgets every group
- [ ] Places: a map with groups of photos and their counts, Open the map

## Controller look (photos-4)
- [ ] LB / RB tabs: For you, All photos, Albums, People, Places, Videos; the photo count
- [ ] a big Memory with A Play as a slideshow and X Browse them
- [ ] rows of big tiles (Today and yesterday, Albums) with names
- [ ] button hints: A Open, X Slideshow, Y Search, ☰ Options, B Back; "shows like this when a controller is in use"

## Edit (photos-5)
- [ ] Cancel, the photo's name, Undo, Redo, Hold to see the original, Replace the original… (asks), Save as a copy (the main button)
- [ ] crop with the frame, its corners and thirds; Straighten with its angle
- [ ] Light / Color / Looks / Fix up tabs, Auto fix, Crop shape
- [ ] Brightness, Contrast, Highlights, Shadows, Warmth, Saturation, Vignette sliders with their values
- [ ] "The original stays as it is …": a copy next to it; replacing asks first and keeps the old one in Trash for 30 days

# Hearth Ideas (approved Nov 22, 2026)

The owner, Nov 22: "Hearth Ideas app would be made for it we would need a way to quickly push the code for idea we
like", then "Mockups look great". On the owner's devices only (the Store's private list), his own GitHub sign-in and his
AI team (the Hearth AI Command Center). Its inbox is desktop-layouts board 10 (checklists/desktop-layouts.md § 10).

Mockups: hearth-ideas/ideas-1-idea-page.png, hearth-ideas/ideas-2-being-built.png, hearth-ideas/ideas-3-ship-it.png
Release: next

## The window
- [ ] Sidebar: New (with its count), Later, Being built, Ready to ship, Shipped, Said no, each with a count
- [ ] "On your devices only. Uses your own GitHub sign-in and your AI team (the Command Center). Nothing goes out without your version number."

## 1. An idea's page
- [ ] ‹ back to the list; the idea's title; its kind (Needs something new / built by people already / has a sketch); Later, No
- [ ] The part and app, how many asked, first and newest
- [ ] What people asked, in their words (the same idea once, the rest counted: "and 20 more · 2 with a sketch")
- [ ] What Describe it made instead, and how many kept it
- [ ] Build it with your AI team: what the Command Center gets, the repo and its own branch, the lead and the checking AI (Change ▾), Draw a mockup first, Build it
- [ ] Or put it on the roadmap: adds it to docs/ROADMAP.md with the people's words

## 2. Being built
- [ ] Title, Building, Open in the Command Center, Stop
- [ ] The steps, each ticked, running or waiting: sent to the AI team (who leads, who checks, when), branch made, built and tested in GitHub Actions (the tests), pictures being taken, pull request to bughunt, ready to ship
- [ ] Its pictures so far (CI's, with their names and times)
- [ ] What the AI team says about it

## 3. Ship it
- [ ] Ready to ship; CI green, pictures match the mockup, at rest (its CPU), the pull request looked at
- [ ] Goes out as: a quick update (Hearth Store) or in the next Hearth OS update
- [ ] What updates; for which Hearth OS
- [ ] Version number: empty, "Hearth never picks it"; Ship it lights up only once it's filled in
- [ ] What's new (filled in from the idea, can be changed)
- [ ] Tell who asked (the people who asked, in their Hearth Store)
- [ ] What happens when you press Ship: into bughunt, the quick update built (Actions › Quick update), added to the Store's list, then Publish as a second press
- [ ] Not yet, Ship it

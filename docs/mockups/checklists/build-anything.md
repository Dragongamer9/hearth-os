## 1. No two desktops are the same
- [ ] Any desktop made from blocks, each person's own, on the same Hearth (apps, games, updates, settings)
- [ ] Ready-made starting points: console dashboard, icon-row home, starship cockpit, cabin by the fire, paper notebook, 1995 computer
- [ ] Each made by saying it, sketching it or building it block by block; the words that made it kept with it
- [ ] Only Hearth's own icons, sounds and fonts in them: no other company's logos, names or sounds

## 2. The desktop as a console dashboard
- [ ] Whole-screen layouts (not only bars, docks and panels), with windows, Alt+Tab and Files working over them
- [ ] Profile corner (picture, name, online, friends playing); search, settings, battery and time corner
- [ ] Hero tile of the last game that grows on focus with a white outline, with when and where you were
- [ ] Rows: Jump back in (games), Your apps and things (Games, Store, Files, Browser, Music, Friends, downloads)
- [ ] The Guide: a side menu on the Home button (Home, Quick settings, Friends and chat, Capture and share, Your windows, Game Mode, Power)

## 3. The desktop as an icon-row home
- [ ] Tabs across the top (Games, Media, Desktop: Desktop shows your windows and files)
- [ ] An icon row, the picked one bigger with its outline; the library at the end
- [ ] The picked game's art filling the screen, its name big, Play and ⋯
- [ ] Activity cards under it (best lap, played this week, next up)

## 4. The block editor
- [ ] Blocks to drag onto the screen: Hero tile, Tile row, Icon row, Tabs, Guide menu, Clock, Profile, Battery, Bar, Dock, Media, Friends, Notes, Weather, Windows, Your own…
- [ ] "Describe a block that isn't here, and your AI makes it"; Made by people
- [ ] Free placement on a grid, any size, with handles; the picked block outlined
- [ ] The picked block: Shows; Look (colours, Corners, See-through, Font, On focus, Sound, Animation); Place (grid, above or under windows); When pressed
- [ ] Undo, Try it full screen, Share it, Keep this; Describe it under the screen

## 5. A block your AI makes
- [ ] New blocks written by your AI when Hearth hasn't got one, shown live as NEW
- [ ] Each runs as its own small program: stopped by itself if it uses too much processor or memory; can't touch starting up, Hearth's system or your settings; off any time in Settings › Your desktop › Your blocks
- [ ] It asks before it reaches anything (an address, files, the internet): Allow, Just this once, No
- [ ] What you asked; Keep this, Change it…, Try again, Undo, Share it with Hearth…

## 6. Hearth Game Mode from the same blocks
- [ ] Game Mode's Home, Library and Quick Access made of the same blocks as the desktop, EDITING in the bar
- [ ] Edited with the controller: A pick up / put down, X its look, Y Describe it, B done, LB/RB between Home, Library and Quick Access, the right stick to resize, the left stick to move
- [ ] Describe it on the on-screen keyboard or by voice (Say it), Undo, Make it
- [ ] A + to add a block; the 15 Home layouts as ready-made starting points

## 7. The first start after a fresh install
- [ ] Full screen, once: "How do you want your desktop?", changeable later in Settings › Your desktop
- [ ] Hearth OS Mantel (Ready now; the bar, the dock, Quick Access, Game Mode): Use Hearth OS Mantel
- [ ] Build your own desktop: the ready-made ones and Start from nothing; Build my own, Describe it
- [ ] Mouse, keyboard, touch or controller (A choose, ◀ ▶ move)

The owner's choices: KDE and GNOME are downloaded only when picked (not in the image); Hearth lives inside their own
look; each desktop shows only its own apps plus the ones you installed (Hearth's own apps stay in Mantel); Mantel is
always built in and is where Hearth goes back to. No KDE or GNOME logos are drawn.

- [ ] Mantel (Hearth Classic): picked, "built in · using now", its line

- [ ] GNOME: its line, "downloading" tag, a progress bar with the percent, "made for Hearth OS <version>", "the rest keeps working while it downloads", Cancel

- [ ] Pick at the login screen too: its line, a "This one ▾" drop-down
- [ ] Safe start: its line, "always on" (twice failing to start opens Mantel and says why; the choice stays saved)

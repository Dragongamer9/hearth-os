Supported ARM devices

Hearth OS is coming to ARM (arm64) tablets and phones, replacing Android with the same Hearth you know: Mantel on the desk, Game Mode on the couch. Here is every device it's being made for and how far along each one is.

Devices

Each device says what works today. None of them has a download yet.

Oukitel RT8

11-inch rugged tablet: MediaTek Helio G99, 6 GB of memory, 256 GB of storage and a 20,000 mAh battery.

Status: working, being polished

The first ARM device. Hearth's desktop runs on it today, and the small things are being fixed now. If Hearth ever can't come up, the tablet falls back by itself to a working system, so it's never left unable to start.

The tablet itself

Wi-Fi, the touch screen, all four speakers, the volume buttons and charging while it's on. The 1200×1920 screen runs in landscape at a scale of 1.25, like a PC.

Hearth on it

Mantel, Hearth's desktop, with Settings, Files, Calculator, Notes and the other apps. Hearth Browser (Firefox for ARM) and Bazaar, the app store, with its full catalog. Sound and its volume slider are remembered across restarts.

Not working yet

Moving the Browser and Bazaar windows by dragging, graphics acceleration (apps draw in software for now), the battery level right after starting, charging while it's off, and the pen.

Coming later

Steam, through x86 emulation, hasn't been tried yet. Minigalaxy doesn't start for now because of a bug in Minigalaxy itself, not the RT8.

Before you try

It needs an unlocked bootloader and replaces Android. There's no download for it yet; it will be listed here when there is.

How it works

The same Hearth OS, built for ARM.

One Hearth

ARM devices get the same Hearth OS as PCs, built for arm64, with the parts each device needs (its kernel and drivers) added on top.

Safe to try

Like every Hearth update, the previous working system always stays startable.

Android tablets you keep

Not replacing Android? Hearth for Android runs as an app instead, made for the Galaxy Tab A9.

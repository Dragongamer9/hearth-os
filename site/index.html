Game Mode

Made for a controller from the first screen

Fullscreen in gamescope, the compositor SteamOS uses. Steam, Epic, GOG, Xbox Cloud Gaming, Xbox PC games, Beacon and your own games in one library; Epic and GOG install without their launchers.

  • Quick Resume: up to five games paused in memory.
  • Cloud saves for Epic and GOG; your Steam friends list.
  • Profiles with optional PIN and parental controls.
  • Works offline: game art is saved on the device.
  • Hearth Input plays games with no controller support.
  • Nothing injected: anti-cheat has nothing to object to.

Why Hearth left Bazzite, and moving from Hearth OS on Bazzite
  • Too much it doesn't use: Bazzite's Game Mode setup, KDE, tools and services cost space, memory and start-up time.
  • Heavier than it should be: 8 to 10 GB installs, bigger updates, sluggish on Nvidia.
  • Two of everything: a separate Nvidia image and ISO; Anatase is one for every PC.
  • Better handhelds: Handheld Daemon, shown in Hearth's own screens.
  • Hearth OS on Bazzite is archived (0.1.3 was its last update). Move from Settings › Updates › Move to a new Hearth OS; games and saves stay. All editions install from the small installer, Anatase also from the offline USB.

Installing from the ISO
  1. Write it to a USB stick16 GB or more, with Fedora Media Writer or balenaEtcher.
  2. Boot from the USBBoot menu: often F12, F11 or F8. ROG Ally: hold Volume Down at power-on.
  3. Try it outThe live system starts in Hearth with a welcome card. Nothing changes yet.
  4. Choose Install HearthPick a drive and restart when it's done.

Switch from Bazzite, Fedora Atomic or another image-based system

Bazzite, Bluefin, Aurora, Silverblue or Kinoite switch with one command, no USB stick. Your account and files stay, and the old system stays in the boot menu. Beta Experimental: back up first. Ubuntu, CachyOS and Peppermint can't be switched to; use the small installer.

From Hearth OS on Bazzite

Settings › Updates › Move to a new Hearth OS keeps your games, saves and store libraries (the rest of home is set aside for 30 days). Or in Desktop Mode:

pkexec /usr/libexec/nimbus/hearth-admin move-to-new-os

From Bazzite, Bluefin, Aurora or Fedora Atomic

sudo bootc switch ghcr.io/dragongamer9/hearth-os:latest
Older system without bootc, or going back 
sudo rpm-ostree rebase ostree-unverified-registry:ghcr.io/dragongamer9/hearth-os:latest

To go back, pick the previous boot entry or run sudo bootc rollback. Hearth OS updates itself from Settings › Updates.

This is a beta. Hearth OS and Ember aren't ready for everyday use yet and things will break now and then. Updates come out almost daily and install themselves. Keep a backup, and tell us what breaks.
FAQ

Questions

What hardware does it need?

Steam Deck, ROG Ally, Legion Go and most x86-64 desktops and laptops with AMD, Intel or Nvidia (GTX 16 / RTX 20 and newer) graphics, and a 16 GB USB stick. CachyOS needs a 2015-or-newer CPU and Secure Boot off.

Can I go back?

Yes. Updates and switches keep the previous system in the boot menu; pick it, or run sudo bootc rollback.

Does it work with anti-cheat games?

Nothing is injected into games; the overlay is an ordinary window. Couch Co-op doesn't support anti-cheat games.

Something broke. How do I report it?

Send logs from Settings › System or with hearth-send-logs, and tell us what happened.

Gaming first

Every game. One couch. No fuss.

Hearth OS starts in Valve's own Steam Game Mode, the one from the Steam Deck. Everything else you own comes along with it.

Steam Game Mode

Valve's couch screens, the same as the Steam Deck: the library, the store, Quick Access and your controller, from the moment it turns on.

Every store, one library

Hearth Sync puts your GOG, Epic and Battle.net games, emulators and recomps into Steam's library, with their pictures, so you never hunt for a launcher.

Windows games just run New in 1.10

GE-Proton is built in: double-click a .exe or .msi and it opens. And it works fully offline, without Steam.

Mods made easy

Hearth Mod Organizer keeps Mod Organizer 2's layout, and Wabbajack lists install with their downloads handled.

Console classics, native

Recomps: native PC ports of console games, installed and kept up to date in their own tab in Hearth Games.

Game servers in a click

Minecraft, Valheim, Terraria and Palworld servers from Settings, so friends can join while you play.

New in 1.10

Steam Game Mode, or Hearth's own. In Hearth's, Steam games still open in Steam Game Mode, and if Hearth's ever can't start, you land in Steam's instead. Change it any time in Settings › Gaming.

    Offline USB

    Hearth OS on Debian on the stick, so it installs without internet. It updates itself to the newest version after installing.

    Download about 4.3 GB SHA-256 checksum

    Recovery USB

    Keep one in a drawer. It starts the previous version, reinstalls keeping your files, or copies them off.

    Questions

    What hardware does it need?

    Steam Deck, ROG Ally, Legion Go and most x86-64 desktops and laptops with AMD, Intel or Nvidia (GTX 16 / RTX 20 and newer) graphics. For installing, a USB stick of 16 GB or more and a drive of 64 GB or more.

    Do I need a Steam account?

    Steam Game Mode is Valve's Steam client, so you sign in to Steam there. Mantel, Hearth's desktop, works without one.

    Can I go back to the last version?

    Yes. Updates keep the previous system in the boot menu; pick it, or use Settings › System › Updates. A version that fails to start twice in a row goes back by itself, and holding Volume Down while it starts opens Hearth Recovery. If nothing starts, the Recovery USB can start the previous version.

    I'm on Anatase. What now?

    Anatase keeps working on its last version, but gets no more updates. To keep getting updates, install Hearth OS on Debian; games on a separate drive stay.

    Is it really free?

    Yes. Hearth OS is free and open source under the GPL-3.0. Games you buy come from the stores as usual.

    Something broke. How do I report it?

    Send logs from Settings › System › Help & logs or with hearth-send-logs, and tell us what happened.

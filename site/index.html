The Game Mode is Steam's Game Mode

Hearth OS starts into Valve's Steam client in gamescope, the same full-screen gamepad screens SteamOS uses: Steam's library, store, Quick Access menu and overlay. The Hearth plugin for Decky Loader brings Hearth's tools into it.

  • Starts by itself. Turn on the PC or handheld and you're in Steam Game Mode, ready for the controller.
  • Mantel one press away. Switch to the desktop from Steam's power menu, and back again.
  • Hearth inside Steam. Hearth's quick settings, power modes, updates and your non-Steam games, through Decky.
  • Updates from Steam's own settings. Check for updates runs Hearth's update, and the device never restarts by itself.

In 0.6.63 Steam Game Mode is already there as an option to switch to (Experimental). From the next update it is the Game Mode. How Steam Game Mode works

    You turn it on

    The boot menu starts the newest version, and keeps the one before it.

  2. 2

    Steam Game Mode opens

    Your games, from Steam and from Hearth Sync, in one library.

  3. !

    If Steam Game Mode can't start

    The PC opens Mantel instead, with a message saying what happened and a button to try again.

Your gaming PC is also your home server

Share folders with every device in the house, reach them from anywhere through Tailscale, back everything up and run your own media server and AI, all from Settings › Home server, with a controller. The PC keeps playing games: big jobs wait while you play.

  • Shared foldersWindows PCs, phones, TVs and other Hearth devices open them like any network drive. Read only or read & write, with a 30-day recycle bin.
  • Anywhere, through TailscaleA private network for your own devices: open your files from work or school, send files with Taildrop and wake the PC from away.
  • Light mode while you playBackups, snapshots and clean-ups wait while Game Mode or a game runs, and the AI unloads from the graphics card. Sharing keeps working.
  • Off until you turn it onNothing is shared and no server runs until you choose it. The system drive is never shared or formatted.

Drives & undo

Drive health and temperatures, btrfs snapshots to bring back deleted or changed files, and new drives as more space, a mirror or a backup target, with a dry run first.

People

Accounts for the household that can only open shares, each with their own access and an optional folder with a size limit. Removing someone never deletes their files.

Alerts & power

A note when a drive is unhealthy or full, a backup fails or a mirror loses a drive. Sleeps when nobody needs it, never while you play, and wakes on a schedule or over the network.

Services

Jellyfin for movies, shows, music and photos, and files in a browser. Each is downloaded only when you ask, from its maker, with its checksum checked.

Family & home

A Family folder for everyone, and cameras on your network shown once one is found.

Updates that can always go back

Each update replaces the system in one piece and keeps the version before it startable. Three ways back, from the easiest:

1

The boot menu

The previous version stays in the boot menu. Pick it there, or go back from Settings.

2

Automatic fallback

If Hearth fails to start twice in a row, the previous version starts by itself and stays the default.

3

Hearth Recovery

Hold Volume Down while starting, or boot the Recovery USB: reinstall keeping your files, copy files off, send logs.

Questions

What hardware does it need?

Steam Deck, ROG Ally, Legion Go and most x86-64 desktops and laptops with AMD, Intel or Nvidia (GTX 16 / RTX 20 and newer) graphics. For installing, a USB stick of 16 GB or more and a drive of 64 GB or more.

Do I need a Steam account?

Steam Game Mode is Valve's Steam client, so you sign in to Steam there. Mantel, Hearth's desktop, works without one.

Can I go back to the last version?

Yes. Updates keep the previous system in the boot menu; pick it, or use Settings › System › Updates. A version that fails to start twice in a row goes back by itself, and holding Volume Down while it starts opens Hearth Recovery. If nothing starts, the Recovery USB can start the previous version.

I'm on Anatase. What now?

Anatase keeps working on its last version, but gets no more updates. To keep getting updates, install Hearth OS on Debian; games on a separate drive stay.

Is it really free?

Yes. Hearth OS is free and open source under the GPL-3.0. Games you buy come from the stores as usual.

Something broke. How do I report it?

Send logs from Settings › System › Help & logs or with hearth-send-logs, and tell us what happened.

# Game art credits

The pictures in this folder come from open-source games whose art may be shared. They are used in the website's
screenshots of Hearth (ui/index.html's browser preview with `?preview=linux&samples=open`) and are made by
`tools/site-shots/open-art.py` from each project's own repository at a fixed commit. The art is only cropped, resized
and converted to WebP, as noted for each file; nothing else is changed. Each file keeps its original licence, and the
website screenshots that show it are shared under the same terms. The games are trademarks or names of their projects;
Hearth OS is not affiliated with them.

What we do

Everything Hearth

Hearth Organization oversees every Hearth project. Each one is its own part, and each one connects to the rest.

Hearth OS

The free, Debian-based gaming OS: Steam Game Mode on the couch, the Mantel desktop on the desk, a home server mode when you're not playing, and every part of it yours to rebuild.

Hearth apps

The Hearth Store, Files, Settings, Notes, Task Manager, Capture Card, Console Remote Play, the AI Command Center and Ithil, each updating on its own through the Store.

Games

More to come soon.

Tools

Hearth CAD, a 3D design app with a slicer built in; the Beacon launcher for classic strategy games; and our Mod Manager, native on Linux.

How we work

Your PC comes first

Nothing changes by itself

No surprise updates and no settings changing behind your back. Every update waits until you say so, and the version before it is always there to go back to.

Small updates, often

Apps and parts update through the Hearth Store, so fixes reach you in minutes instead of waiting for the next big release.

Make it yours

Your desktop, Game Mode, the lock screen and every Hearth app are made of blocks you can move, resize, hide or describe.

Built with AI

AI helps us with all our projects: writing and testing code, drawing mockups and checking our work. Every change is tested before it ships, and nothing ships until we say so.

Better open source

We take the open-source projects we use and make them better, like our Mod Manager, a native Linux mod manager still in the works. Every one of our own is open source on GitHub. Read it, build it, or send us an idea from inside Hearth.

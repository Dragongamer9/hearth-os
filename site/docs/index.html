What Hearth OS is

Hearth OS is a controller-first gaming system for handhelds and PCs. It starts in Game Mode, a console-style screen with Steam, Epic, GOG, Xbox and your other games in one library, and has a full desktop called Mantel one button away (Desktop Mode).

It's image-based: the whole system is one tested image that updates in one piece, in the background. An update can't half-install, and the version you had before stays on the drive so you can go back to it.

Hearth OS is in beta. It works for everyday play, but expect rough edges. When something breaks, send a log and it gets looked at.

Editions

Hearth looks and works the same on every edition. What changes is the Linux underneath it.

EditionBuilt onStatusGood for
AnataseFedora (Anatase)RecommendedEveryone. The most tested edition, with the best hardware support.
UbuntuUbuntu 26.04Beta ExperimentalPeople who know Ubuntu, or need Ubuntu packages.
CachyOSCachyOS (Arch)Beta ExperimentalNewer processors (x86-64-v3: AVX2). Tuned kernel, handheld patches.
PeppermintDebian 13Beta ExperimentalPeople who prefer Debian's slower, steady pace.

You can switch edition later without a USB stick. Your games and saves come along.

What you need

  • A 64-bit PC or handheld (x86-64) with AMD, Intel or Nvidia graphics (Nvidia GTX 16 / RTX 20 or newer).
  • For installing: a USB stick of 16 GB or more, and a drive of 64 GB or more.
  • CachyOS only: a processor from about 2015 or newer (x86-64-v3).

Tested on the ASUS ROG Ally and Xbox Ally, and desktop PCs with AMD and Nvidia graphics.

Docs · for Hearth OS 0.5.1

Installing

Download Hearth OS, write it to a USB stick, and install it on its own drive or next to Windows.

Downloads

Pick one. The offline USBs carry the whole system and install without internet. The small installer downloads the system while it installs, and offers every edition.

DownloadSizeInternet while installing
Anatase, offline USB (recommended)about 9 GBNot needed. Try it before installing.
Small installer (every edition)1.6 GBNeeded
Ubuntu, offlineabout 4.4 GBNot needed
CachyOS, offlineabout 5.2 GBNot needed
Peppermint, offlineabout 4.3 GBNot needed

To check a download wasn't damaged, compare its checksum (a .sha256 file sits next to each ISO, for example hearth-os-live.iso.sha256). On Linux:

sha256sum hearth-os-live.iso

The result should match the text in the .sha256 file.

Making the USB stick

  1. Get a USB writer. Fedora Media Writer or balenaEtcher, on Windows, macOS or Linux.
  2. Write the ISO to the stick. Choose "custom image" or "flash from file", pick the ISO, pick the stick. Everything on the stick is erased.

Starting from the USB

  1. Plug in the stick and turn the device on while pressing its boot-menu key: often F12, F11 or F8 on PCs. On a ROG Ally: hold Volume Down while pressing power.
  2. Pick the USB stick in the list. If it isn't there, Secure Boot or "Fast Boot" may be hiding it: turn those off in the firmware settings (often Del or F2 at power-on).
  3. Try it out. The offline Anatase USB starts Hearth straight away with a welcome card. Nothing on the drive changes until you choose to install.

Installing

  1. Choose Install Hearth (on the welcome card, or the installer that opens on its own).
  2. Pick where it goes: a whole drive (everything on it is erased), or next to Windows if there's space.
  3. Make your account: name, password, time zone, Wi-Fi.
  4. Wait, then restart when it says it's done, and take the USB stick out.

Every ISO is tested before it's published: it's installed on a virtual PC and the installed system has to start. If an install fails on your hardware, send the installer log: the installer saves one.

The first start

  • Hearth starts in Game Mode. Sign in to Steam and your other stores from Settings › Game stores.
  • Steam installs itself (from Flathub) the first time the device is online, so after an offline install it appears a few minutes after you connect.
  • Steam is already installed (from the edition's own packages).
  • An installed system starts at the version the ISO was made with, and updates itself to the newest one once it's online.

Coming from Bazzite or Fedora Atomic

Bazzite, Bluefin, Aurora, Silverblue and Kinoite can move to Hearth OS without a USB stick; your account and files stay. From the Bazzite-based Hearth OS, use Settings › Updates › Move to a new Hearth OS, or in Desktop Mode:

pkexec /usr/libexec/nimbus/hearth-admin move-to-new-os

Beta Experimental: back up anything important first.
